Our Process

How we work

A thoughtful, transparent journey from first conversation to final home

Renovating or designing your home is one of the most significant investments you'll make. Our process is designed to be clear, collaborative, and free of surprises — so that you feel confident and excited at every stage.

01

Discovery Call

Every project begins with a conversation. We take 30 minutes to hear about your project, your family, your timeline, and your budget. This helps us understand whether we're the right fit — and if we are, what working together might look like.

Step Image
02

Site Visit & Concept

We visit your home, take measurements, and get a feel for the space and the light. From there, we develop an initial concept direction — mood, materials, and a sense of what the finished home could feel like.

Step Image
03

Design Development

We refine the concept into a full design — floor plans, material boards, custom millwork drawings, furniture selections, and full specifications. We present this in a detailed package and work through revisions collaboratively.

Step Image
04

Construction & Procurement

We work alongside your general contractor (or help you find one) to oversee the build, manage trades, and ensure the design intent is carried through every detail. Simultaneously, we coordinate furniture and material orders to keep timelines aligned.

Step Image
05

Installation & Styling

Once construction is complete, we manage the furniture install, art placement, and final styling — the details that transform a finished renovation into a home. We want you to walk in for the first time and feel like it was made exactly for you.

Step Image
