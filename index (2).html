About the Studio

A small studio with a deeply personal practice

Stone & Birch was founded on the belief that your home should feel like the most considered expression of who you are. We take on a limited number of projects each year so that every client receives our full attention.

Based in Vancouver, we work throughout British Columbia and beyond for the right project.
