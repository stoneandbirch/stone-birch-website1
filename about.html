About

Thoughtful design rooted in how you live

Stone & Birch is a full-service interior design studio based in Vancouver, BC. We were founded in 2014 with a simple belief: that great design should feel personal, liveable, and enduring.

We work closely with each client from the first conversation through the final install, taking on a carefully selected number of projects each year so that every home gets our full attention, creativity, and care.

Our work spans full home renovations, new builds, condo transformations, and decoration projects — always with the same commitment to craft, honesty, and design that truly fits your life.

We believe the best homes are not designed to impress — they are designed to be lived in. Warmly, fully, and without reservation.
